# Relationships

Common English expressions used to describe mathematical relationships, dependencies, trends, and comparisons.

| English                           | Japanese Meaning  | Example                                           |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **is equal to**                   | …に等しい             | \(x\) is equal to \(5\).                          |
| **is greater than**               | …より大きい            | \(x\) is greater than \(y\).                      |
| **is less than**                  | …より小さい            | \(x\) is less than \(y\).                         |
| **is proportional to**            | …に比例する            | \(y\) is proportional to \(x\).                   |
| **is inversely proportional to**  | …に反比例する           | \(y\) is inversely proportional to \(x\).         |
| **depends on**                    | …に依存する            | The output depends on the input.                  |
| **is related to**                 | …と関係している          | The variable is related to income.                |
| **is a function of**              | …の関数である           | \(y\) is a function of \(x\).                     |
| **increases with**                | …とともに増加する         | \(y\) increases with \(x\).                       |
| **decreases with**                | …とともに減少する         | The error decreases with the sample size.         |
| **increases as**                  | …するにつれて増加する       | The probability increases as \(x\) increases.     |
| **decreases as**                  | …するにつれて減少する       | The loss decreases as training progresses.        |
| **is positively correlated with** | …と正の相関がある         | Income is positively correlated with education.   |
| **is negatively correlated with** | …と負の相関がある         | Price is negatively correlated with demand.       |
| **is independent of**             | …から独立している／…に依存しない | \(Y\) is independent of \(X\).                    |
| **is dependent on**               | …に依存している          | The result is dependent on the initial condition. |
| **is greater than or equal to**   | …以上である            | \(x\) is greater than or equal to \(0\).          |
| **is less than or equal to**      | …以下である            | \(x\) is less than or equal to \(1\).             |
| **approaches**                    | …に近づく             | \(f(x)\) approaches \(1\) as \(x\to\infty\).      |
| **converges to**                  | …に収束する            | The sequence converges to \(0\).                  |
| **diverges to**                   | …へ発散する            | The sequence diverges to infinity.                |
| **is approximately equal to**     | …とほぼ等しい           | \(e\) is approximately equal to \(2.718\).        |
| **is equivalent to**              | …と同値である           | The two expressions are equivalent.               |
| **is consistent with**            | …と整合する            | The result is consistent with the theory.         |

## Examples in Context

### Is proportional to

> The force is proportional to the acceleration.

**Japanese:**
力は加速度に比例する。

---

### Is inversely proportional to

> The pressure is inversely proportional to the volume.

**Japanese:**
圧力は体積に反比例する。

---

### Depends on

> The prediction accuracy depends on the quality of the data.

**Japanese:**
予測精度はデータの品質に依存する。

---

### Is a function of

> The output is a function of the input variables.

**Japanese:**
出力は入力変数の関数である。

---

### Increases as

> The probability increases as the sample size increases.

**Japanese:**
標本サイズが増加するにつれて、確率は増加する。

---

### Decreases as

> The loss decreases as the model learns from the data.

**Japanese:**
モデルがデータから学習するにつれて、損失は減少する。

---

### Approaches

> As \(x\) increases, \(f(x)\) approaches \(1\).

**Japanese:**
\(x\) が増加すると、\(f(x)\) は1に近づく。

---

### Converges to

> The sequence converges to \(0\).

**Japanese:**
その数列は0に収束する。

---

### Is approximately equal to

> The estimated value is approximately equal to \(3.14\).

**Japanese:**
推定値は約3.14に等しい。

---

## Mathematical Relationships

### Direct Proportionality

> \(y\) is proportional to \(x\).

$$
y \propto x
$$

**Japanese:**
\(y\) は \(x\) に比例する。

---

### Inverse Proportionality

> \(y\) is inversely proportional to \(x\).

$$
y \propto \frac{1}{x}
$$

**Japanese:**
\(y\) は \(x\) に反比例する。

---

### Functional Relationship

> \(y\) is a function of \(x\).

$$
y=f(x)
$$

**Japanese:**
\(y\) は \(x\) の関数である。

---

### Independence

> \(Y\) is independent of \(X\).

$$
Y \perp X
$$

**Japanese:**
\(Y\) は \(X\) から独立している。

---
