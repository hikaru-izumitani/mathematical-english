# Economics — Fundamental Formulas and Concepts

This file provides a mathematical and conceptual foundation for economics.
It focuses on fundamental formulas and terms that appear across microeconomics, macroeconomics, econometrics, and finance.

| English                                 | 日本語           | Formula                                                                         | Meaning                                                                                                                                 |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Economics                               | 経済学           | —                                                                               | The study of how people and organizations allocate scarce resources.                                                                    |
| Scarcity                                | 希少性           | —                                                                               | Resources are limited while human wants are unlimited.                                                                                  |
| Opportunity Cost                        | 機会費用          | —                                                                               | The value of the next-best alternative that is given up.                                                                                |
| Marginal Analysis                       | 限界分析          | —                                                                               | Analyzing the additional benefit and cost of one more unit of an activity.                                                              |
| Marginal Benefit                        | 限界便益          | \(MB = \frac{\Delta TB}{\Delta Q}\)                                             | The additional benefit from one more unit.                                                                                              |
| Marginal Cost                           | 限界費用          | \(MC = \frac{\Delta TC}{\Delta Q}\)                                             | The additional cost from one more unit.                                                                                                 |
| Total Benefit                           | 総便益           | \(TB = \sum MB\)                                                                | The total benefit obtained from an activity.                                                                                            |
| Total Cost                              | 総費用           | \(TC = FC + VC\)                                                                | The total cost of production or an activity.                                                                                            |
| Average                                 | 平均            | \(\bar{x} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i\)                                      | The sum of observations divided by the number of observations.                                                                          |
| Percentage Change                       | 変化率           | \(\frac{X_1-X_0}{X_0}\times100\)                                                | The percentage change from an initial value to a new value.                                                                             |
| Growth Rate                             | 成長率           | \(g = \frac{X_t-X_{t-1}}{X_{t-1}}\)                                             | The rate at which a variable increases or decreases over time.                                                                          |
| Weighted Average                        | 加重平均          | \(\bar{x}_w = \sum_i w_i x_i\)                                                  | An average in which observations receive different weights.                                                                             |
| Equilibrium                             | 均衡            | \(D = S\)                                                                       | A state in which opposing economic forces are balanced.                                                                                 |
| Efficiency                              | 効率性           | —                                                                               | A situation in which resources are allocated without unnecessary waste.                                                                 |
| Economic Surplus                        | 経済余剰          | \(TS = CS + PS\)                                                                | The combined surplus received by consumers and producers.                                                                               |
| Consumer Surplus                        | 消費者余剰         | \(CS = \text{Willingness to Pay} - \text{Price}\)                               | The difference between what consumers are willing to pay and what they actually pay.                                                    |
| Producer Surplus                        | 生産者余剰         | \(PS = \text{Price} - \text{Minimum Acceptable Price}\)                         | The difference between the price received and the minimum price at which producers would sell.                                          |
| Deadweight Loss                         | 死荷重           | —                                                                               | The loss of total economic surplus caused by an inefficient allocation of resources.                                                    |
| Elasticity                              | 弾力性           | \(E = \frac{\%\Delta Y}{\%\Delta X}\)                                           | Measures how responsive one variable is to a percentage change in another variable.                                                     |
| Price Elasticity                        | 価格弾力性         | \(E_P = \frac{\%\Delta Q}{\%\Delta P}\)                                         | Measures how quantity responds to a change in price.                                                                                    |
| Income Elasticity                       | 所得弾力性         | \(E_I = \frac{\%\Delta Q}{\%\Delta I}\)                                         | Measures how quantity demanded responds to a change in income.                                                                          |
| Cross-Price Elasticity                  | 交差価格弾力性       | \(E_{XY} = \frac{\%\Delta Q_X}{\%\Delta P_Y}\)                                  | Measures how demand for one good responds to the price of another good.                                                                 |
| Marginal Utility                        | 限界効用          | \(MU = \frac{\Delta U}{\Delta Q}\)                                              | The additional utility obtained from consuming one more unit.                                                                           |
| Utility                                 | 効用            | \(U = U(x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n)\)                                                   | A representation of the satisfaction or preferences associated with consumption.                                                        |
| Production Function                     | 生産関数          | \(Q = f(K,L)\)                                                                  | Describes the maximum output that can be produced from given inputs.                                                                    |
| Capital                                 | 資本            | \(K\)                                                                           | Produced assets used to produce goods and services.                                                                                     |
| Labor                                   | 労働            | \(L\)                                                                           | Human effort used in the production of goods and services.                                                                              |
| Marginal Product                        | 限界生産物         | \(MP_X = \frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta X}\)                                            | The additional output generated by one additional unit of an input.                                                                     |
| Average Product                         | 平均生産物         | \(AP_X = \frac{Q}{X}\)                                                          | Output produced per unit of an input.                                                                                                   |
| Total Revenue                           | 総収入           | \(TR = P\times Q\)                                                              | The total revenue received from selling a quantity of goods or services.                                                                |
| Average Revenue                         | 平均収入          | \(AR = \frac{TR}{Q}\)                                                           | Revenue received per unit of output.                                                                                                    |
| Marginal Revenue                        | 限界収入          | \(MR = \frac{\Delta TR}{\Delta Q}\)                                             | The additional revenue generated by selling one more unit.                                                                              |
| Profit                                  | 利潤            | \(\pi = TR - TC\)                                                               | Revenue minus total cost.                                                                                                               |
| Average Cost                            | 平均費用          | \(AC = \frac{TC}{Q}\)                                                           | Total cost per unit of output.                                                                                                          |
| Fixed Cost                              | 固定費           | \(FC\)                                                                          | A cost that does not change with the level of output in the short run.                                                                  |
| Variable Cost                           | 可変費用          | \(VC\)                                                                          | A cost that changes with the level of output.                                                                                           |
| Average Fixed Cost                      | 平均固定費用        | \(AFC = \frac{FC}{Q}\)                                                          | Fixed cost per unit of output.                                                                                                          |
| Average Variable Cost                   | 平均可変費用        | \(AVC = \frac{VC}{Q}\)                                                          | Variable cost per unit of output.                                                                                                       |
| Marginal Cost                           | 限界費用          | \(MC = \frac{\Delta TC}{\Delta Q}\)                                             | The additional cost of producing one more unit.                                                                                         |
| Budget Constraint                       | 予算制約          | \(P_xX + P_yY = I\)                                                             | Describes the combinations of goods a consumer can afford given prices and income.                                                      |
| Marginal Rate of Substitution           | 限界代替率         | \(MRS_{XY} = \frac{MU_X}{MU_Y}\)                                                | The amount of one good a consumer is willing to give up for another while maintaining the same utility.                                 |
| Marginal Rate of Technical Substitution | 限界技術代替率       | \(MRTS_{LK} = \frac{MP_L}{MP_K}\)                                               | The rate at which one input can replace another while maintaining the same level of output.                                             |
| Supply                                  | 供給            | \(Q_s = f(P,\ldots)\)                                                           | The quantity producers are willing and able to sell at different prices.                                                                |
| Demand                                  | 需要            | \(Q_d = f(P,\ldots)\)                                                           | The quantity consumers are willing and able to purchase at different prices.                                                            |
| Demand Function                         | 需要関数          | \(Q_d = a-bP\)                                                                  | A function describing the relationship between quantity demanded and its determinants.                                                  |
| Supply Function                         | 供給関数          | \(Q_s = c+dP\)                                                                  | A function describing the relationship between quantity supplied and its determinants.                                                  |
| Market Equilibrium                      | 市場均衡          | \(Q_d = Q_s\)                                                                   | The point where quantity demanded equals quantity supplied.                                                                             |
| Nominal Value                           | 名目値           | —                                                                               | A value measured using current prices without adjusting for changes in the price level.                                                 |
| Real Value                              | 実質値           | —                                                                               | A value adjusted for changes in the price level.                                                                                        |
| Inflation                               | インフレーション      | \(\pi = \frac{P_t-P_{t-1}}{P_{t-1}}\times100\)                                  | A sustained increase in the general price level.                                                                                        |
| Deflation                               | デフレーション       | —                                                                               | A sustained decrease in the general price level.                                                                                        |
| Consumer Price Index                    | 消費者物価指数       | \(CPI = \frac{\text{Cost of Basket}_t}{\text{Cost of Basket}_{base}}\times100\) | An index measuring the price level of a representative basket of consumer goods and services.                                           |
| Inflation Rate                          | インフレ率         | \(\frac{CPI_t-CPI_{t-1}}{CPI_{t-1}}\times100\)                                  | The percentage change in the price level over time.                                                                                     |
| Gross Domestic Product                  | 国内総生産         | \(GDP = C+I+G+NX\)                                                              | The market value of final goods and services produced within a country's borders.                                                       |
| Consumption                             | 消費            | \(C\)                                                                           | Household spending on goods and services.                                                                                               |
| Investment                              | 投資            | \(I\)                                                                           | Spending on capital goods, inventories, and other productive assets.                                                                    |
| Government Spending                     | 政府支出          | \(G\)                                                                           | Government purchases of goods and services.                                                                                             |
| Net Exports                             | 純輸出           | \(NX = X-M\)                                                                    | Exports minus imports.                                                                                                                  |
| Exports                                 | 輸出            | \(X\)                                                                           | Goods and services sold to other countries.                                                                                             |
| Imports                                 | 輸入            | \(M\)                                                                           | Goods and services purchased from other countries.                                                                                      |
| GDP Deflator                            | GDPデフレーター     | \(\frac{Nominal\ GDP}{Real\ GDP}\times100\)                                     | An index measuring the price level of domestically produced final goods and services.                                                   |
| Real GDP                                | 実質GDP         | —                                                                               | GDP measured using constant or base-period prices.                                                                                      |
| Nominal GDP                             | 名目GDP         | —                                                                               | GDP measured using current market prices.                                                                                               |
| Economic Growth                         | 経済成長          | \(g = \frac{Y_t-Y_{t-1}}{Y_{t-1}}\times100\)                                    | An increase in an economy's real output or productive capacity over time.                                                               |
| Compound Growth                         | 複利成長          | \(Y_t=Y_0(1+g)^t\)                                                              | Growth in which each period's increase is based on the accumulated previous value.                                                      |
| Rule of 70                              | 70の法則         | \(Doubling\ Time\approx\frac{70}{g}\)                                           | An approximation of the number of years required for a quantity to double at a constant growth rate.                                    |
| Unemployment Rate                       | 失業率           | \(\frac{Unemployed}{Labor\ Force}\times100\)                                    | The percentage of the labor force that is unemployed.                                                                                   |
| Labor Force                             | 労働力人口         | \(LF=E+U\)                                                                      | The number of employed and unemployed people actively participating in the labor market.                                                |
| Labor Force Participation Rate          | 労働参加率         | \(\frac{Labor\ Force}{Working\text{-}Age\ Population}\times100\)                | The percentage of the working-age population that participates in the labor force.                                                      |
| Real Interest Rate                      | 実質金利          | \(r\approx i-\pi\)                                                              | The nominal interest rate adjusted approximately for inflation.                                                                         |
| Fisher Equation                         | フィッシャー方程式     | \(1+i=(1+r)(1+\pi)\)                                                            | Relates the nominal interest rate, real interest rate, and inflation rate.                                                              |
| Money Supply                            | 貨幣供給量         | \(M\)                                                                           | The quantity of money available in an economy.                                                                                          |
| Velocity of Money                       | 貨幣流通速度        | \(V=\frac{PY}{M}\)                                                              | The average number of times a unit of money is used to purchase final goods and services during a period.                               |
| Quantity Equation                       | 貨幣数量方程式       | \(MV=PY\)                                                                       | An identity relating money supply, velocity, price level, and real output.                                                              |
| Disposable Income                       | 可処分所得         | \(Y_d=Y-T+TR\)                                                                  | Income available to households after taxes and including transfers.                                                                     |
| Consumption Function                    | 消費関数          | \(C=C_0+cY_d\)                                                                  | Describes consumption as a function of disposable income.                                                                               |
| Marginal Propensity to Consume          | 限界消費性向        | \(MPC=\frac{\Delta C}{\Delta Y_d}\)                                             | The fraction of an additional unit of disposable income that is consumed.                                                               |
| Marginal Propensity to Save             | 限界貯蓄性向        | \(MPS=\frac{\Delta S}{\Delta Y_d}\)                                             | The fraction of an additional unit of disposable income that is saved.                                                                  |
| MPC and MPS                             | 限界消費性向と限界貯蓄性向 | \(MPC+MPS=1\)                                                                   | The entire additional unit of disposable income is either consumed or saved.                                                            |
| Keynesian Multiplier                    | ケインズ乗数        | \(k=\frac{1}{1-MPC}\)                                                           | Measures the total change in aggregate output resulting from an initial autonomous change in spending under a simple model.             |
| Trade Balance                           | 貿易収支          | \(TB=X-M\)                                                                      | The value of exports minus the value of imports.                                                                                        |
| Current Account                         | 経常収支          | —                                                                               | A measure of international transactions involving goods, services, income, and transfers.                                               |
| Fiscal Policy                           | 財政政策          | —                                                                               | Government decisions concerning spending, taxation, and transfers.                                                                      |
| Monetary Policy                         | 金融政策          | —                                                                               | Central-bank actions affecting money, interest rates, and financial conditions.                                                         |
| Aggregate Demand                        | 総需要           | \(AD=C+I+G+NX\)                                                                 | Total planned spending on domestically produced goods and services.                                                                     |
| Aggregate Supply                        | 総供給           | \(AS\)                                                                          | The total quantity of goods and services firms are willing to produce at different price levels.                                        |
| Economic Multiplier                     | 経済乗数          | \(k=\frac{\Delta Y}{\Delta A}\)                                                 | Measures the change in aggregate output relative to an initial autonomous change in spending.                                           |
| Present Value                           | 現在価値          | \(PV=\frac{FV}{(1+r)^t}\)                                                       | The current value of a future amount discounted at an interest rate.                                                                    |
| Future Value                            | 将来価値          | \(FV=PV(1+r)^t\)                                                                | The future value of a current amount after earning a given rate of return.                                                              |
| Discount Rate                           | 割引率           | \(r\)                                                                           | The rate used to convert future cash flows into present values.                                                                         |
| Net Present Value                       | 正味現在価値        | \(NPV=\sum_{t=0}^{T}\frac{CF_t}{(1+r)^t}\)                                      | The present value of future cash flows minus the initial investment.                                                                    |
| Internal Rate of Return                 | 内部収益率         | \(0=\sum_{t=0}^{T}\frac{CF_t}{(1+IRR)^t}\)                                      | The discount rate that makes the net present value equal to zero.                                                                       |
| Comparative Advantage                   | 比較優位          | —                                                                               | The ability to produce a good at a lower opportunity cost than another producer.                                                        |
| Absolute Advantage                      | 絶対優位          | —                                                                               | The ability to produce more output with the same resources or the same output with fewer resources.                                     |
| Trade-off                               | トレードオフ        | —                                                                               | A situation in which obtaining more of one thing requires giving up some of another.                                                    |
| Marginal Benefit–Marginal Cost Rule     | 限界便益・限界費用ルール  | \(MB=MC\)                                                                       | A standard condition for choosing the efficient quantity of an activity when marginal benefit and marginal cost determine the decision. |
| Profit Maximization                     | 利潤最大化         | \(MR=MC\)                                                                       | A standard condition for maximizing profit for an interior solution under conventional differentiability assumptions.                   |
| Returns to Scale                        | 規模に関する収穫      | \(f(\lambda K,\lambda L)\)                                                      | Describes how output changes when all inputs are increased proportionally.                                                              |
| Increasing Returns to Scale             | 規模に関する収穫逓増    | \(f(\lambda K,\lambda L)>\lambda f(K,L)\)                                       | Output increases by more than the proportional increase in all inputs.                                                                  |
| Constant Returns to Scale               | 規模に関する収穫一定    | \(f(\lambda K,\lambda L)=\lambda f(K,L)\)                                       | Output increases in the same proportion as all inputs.                                                                                  |
| Decreasing Returns to Scale             | 規模に関する収穫逓減    | \(f(\lambda K,\lambda L)<\lambda f(K,L)\)                                       | Output increases by less than the proportional increase in all inputs.                                                                  |

## Common Symbols

| ----------- | ----------------------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| \(Q\)       | Quantity                | 数量       | Quantity of a good or service                                                           |
| \(Q_d\)     | Quantity Demanded       | 需要量      | Quantity consumers demand                                                               |
| \(Q_s\)     | Quantity Supplied       | 供給量      | Quantity producers supply                                                               |
| \(Y\)       | Output / Income         | 産出量・所得   | Aggregate output or income, depending on context                                        |
| \(C\)       | Consumption             | 消費       | Household consumption                                                                   |
| \(I\)       | Investment              | 投資       | Investment spending                                                                     |
| \(G\)       | Government Spending     | 政府支出     | Government purchases                                                                    |
| \(X\)       | Exports                 | 輸出       | Goods and services sold abroad                                                          |
| \(M\)       | Imports                 | 輸入       | Goods and services purchased from abroad                                                |
| \(NX\)      | Net Exports             | 純輸出      | Exports minus imports                                                                   |
| \(K\)       | Capital                 | 資本       | Capital input                                                                           |
| \(L\)       | Labor                   | 労働       | Labor input                                                                             |
| \(T\)       | Taxes                   | 税        | Taxes paid to the government                                                            |
| \(TR\)      | Transfers               | 移転支出     | Government transfer payments                                                            |
| \(r\)       | Real Interest Rate      | 実質金利     | Interest rate adjusted for inflation                                                    |
| \(i\)       | Nominal Interest Rate   | 名目金利     | Interest rate before inflation adjustment                                               |
| \(\pi\)     | Inflation Rate / Profit | インフレ率・利潤 | Usually inflation in macroeconomics; profit in microeconomics                           |
| \(U\)       | Utility / Unemployment  | 効用・失業    | Utility in microeconomics; unemployment may be denoted differently depending on context |
| \(MB\)      | Marginal Benefit        | 限界便益     | Additional benefit                                                                      |
| \(MC\)      | Marginal Cost           | 限界費用     | Additional cost                                                                         |
| \(MR\)      | Marginal Revenue        | 限界収入     | Additional revenue                                                                      |
| \(MP\)      | Marginal Product        | 限界生産物    | Additional output from an additional input                                              |
| \(FC\)      | Fixed Cost              | 固定費      | Cost independent of output in the short run                                             |
| \(VC\)      | Variable Cost           | 可変費      | Cost that varies with output                                                            |
| \(TC\)      | Total Cost              | 総費用      | Fixed cost plus variable cost                                                           |
| \(TR\)      | Total Revenue           | 総収入      | Price multiplied by quantity                                                            |
| \(\bar{x}\) | Mean                    | 平均       | Arithmetic average                                                                      |
| \(g\)       | Growth Rate             | 成長率      | Rate of growth                                                                          |
| \(E\)       | Elasticity              | 弾力性      | Responsiveness of one variable to another                                               |
