# Microeconomics — Fundamental Formulas and Concepts

This file provides a mathematical foundation for microeconomics.
It focuses on consumer behavior, demand, supply, production, costs, market structures, welfare, and game theory.

---

## 1. Demand and Supply

| English                 | 日本語   | Formula                             | Meaning                                                            |
| ----------------------- | ----- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Demand                  | 需要    | \(Q_d=f(P,Y,P_s,P_c,T,\ldots)\)     | The quantity of a good consumers are willing and able to purchase. |
| Supply                  | 供給    | \(Q_s=f(P,W,C,T,\ldots)\)           | The quantity of a good producers are willing and able to sell.     |
| Demand Function         | 需要関数  | \(Q_d=a-bP\)                        | A simple linear relationship between price and quantity demanded.  |
| Supply Function         | 供給関数  | \(Q_s=c+dP\)                        | A simple linear relationship between price and quantity supplied.  |
| Inverse Demand Function | 逆需要関数 | \(P=P(Q)\)                          | Expresses price as a function of quantity demanded.                |
| Inverse Supply Function | 逆供給関数 | \(P=P(Q)\)                          | Expresses price as a function of quantity supplied.                |
| Market Equilibrium      | 市場均衡  | \(Q_d=Q_s\)                         | The point where quantity demanded equals quantity supplied.        |
| Equilibrium Price       | 均衡価格  | \(P^*:Q_d(P^*)=Q_s(P^*)\)           | The price at which the market clears.                              |
| Equilibrium Quantity    | 均衡数量  | \(Q^*:Q_d(P^*)=Q_s(P^*)\)           | The quantity traded at market equilibrium.                         |
| Excess Demand           | 超過需要  | \(Q_d>Q_s\)                         | A situation in which quantity demanded exceeds quantity supplied.  |
| Excess Supply           | 超過供給  | \(Q_s>Q_d\)                         | A situation in which quantity supplied exceeds quantity demanded.  |
| Price Ceiling           | 価格上限  | \(P\leq P_{\max}\)                  | A legal maximum price.                                             |
| Price Floor             | 価格下限  | \(P\geq P_{\min}\)                  | A legal minimum price.                                             |
| Consumer Surplus        | 消費者余剰 | \(CS=\int_0^{Q^*}[P_D(Q)-P^*]\,dQ\) | The area between willingness to pay and the market price.          |
| Producer Surplus        | 生産者余剰 | \(PS=\int_0^{Q^*}[P^*-P_S(Q)]\,dQ\) | The area between the market price and the supply curve.            |
| Total Surplus           | 総余剰   | \(TS=CS+PS\)                        | The total economic surplus generated by a market.                  |
| Deadweight Loss         | 死荷重   | \(DWL=TS^*-TS\)                     | The loss of total surplus caused by an inefficient allocation.     |

| --------------------------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Price Elasticity of Demand  | 需要の価格弾力性 | \(E_d=\frac{\%\Delta Q_d}{\%\Delta P}\)         | Measures how responsive quantity demanded is to price.                                                                                                                                |                    |                                                                                 |
| Point Elasticity            | 点弾力性     | \(E_d=\frac{dQ}{dP}\frac{P}{Q}\)                | Measures elasticity at a specific point on a demand curve.                                                                                                                            |                    |                                                                                 |

| Cross-Price Elasticity      | 交差価格弾力性  | \(E_{XY}=\frac{\%\Delta Q_X}{\%\Delta P_Y}\)    | Measures how demand for one good responds to another good's price.                                                                                                                    |                    |                                                                                 |
| Price Elasticity of Supply  | 供給の価格弾力性 | \(E_s=\frac{\%\Delta Q_s}{\%\Delta P}\)         | Measures how responsive quantity supplied is to price.                                                                                                                                |                    |                                                                                 |
| Elastic Demand              | 弾力的需要    | (                                               | E_d                                                                                                                                                                                   | >1)                | Quantity demanded changes proportionally more than price.                       |

| Unit Elastic Demand         | 単位弾力的需要  | (                                               | E_d                                                                                                                                                                                   | =1)                | Quantity and price change by the same percentage.                               |
| Perfectly Elastic Demand    | 完全弾力的需要  | (                                               | E_d                                                                                                                                                                                   | \rightarrow\infty) | An arbitrarily small price increase can reduce quantity demanded substantially. |
| Perfectly Inelastic Demand  | 完全非弾力的需要 | \(E_d=0\)                                       | Quantity demanded does not respond to price.                                                                                                                                          |                    |                                                                                 |
| Total Revenue               | 総収入      | \(TR=P\times Q\)                                | Revenue received from selling output.                                                                                                                                                 |                    |                                                                                 |
| Revenue and Elasticity      | 収入と弾力性   | —                                               | When demand is elastic, a price decrease tends to increase total revenue; when demand is inelastic, a price increase tends to increase total revenue, holding other factors constant. |                    |                                                                                 |

## 3. Consumer Theory

| English                             | 日本語        | Formula                                        | Meaning                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Utility                             | 効用         | \(U=U(x,y)\)                                   | Represents a consumer's preferences over consumption bundles.                                                    |
| Total Utility                       | 総効用        | \(TU=U(Q)\)                                    | Total satisfaction represented by consumption.                                                                   |
| Marginal Utility                    | 限界効用       | \(MU_x=\frac{\partial U}{\partial x}\)         | The additional utility from one more unit of a good.                                                             |
| Average Utility                     | 平均効用       | \(AU=\frac{U}{Q}\)                             | Utility per unit of consumption.                                                                                 |
| Law of Diminishing Marginal Utility | 限界効用逓減の法則  | \(\frac{\partial MU}{\partial Q}<0\)           | Marginal utility tends to decrease as consumption increases, under the standard model.                           |
| Budget Constraint                   | 予算制約       | \(P_xx+P_yy=I\)                                | Describes affordable combinations of goods.                                                                      |
| Budget Line                         | 予算線        | \(y=\frac{I}{P_y}-\frac{P_x}{P_y}x\)           | The set of consumption bundles that exhaust the consumer's budget.                                               |
| Budget Line Slope                   | 予算線の傾き     | \(-\frac{P_x}{P_y}\)                           | The market trade-off between two goods.                                                                          |
| Consumer Optimization               | 消費者最適化     | \(\max U(x,y)\) subject to \(P_xx+P_yy\leq I\) | Finds the utility-maximizing affordable consumption bundle.                                                      |
| Marginal Rate of Substitution       | 限界代替率      | \(MRS_{xy}=\frac{MU_x}{MU_y}\)                 | The amount of \(y\) a consumer is willing to give up for one additional unit of \(x\) while maintaining utility. |
| Interior Optimum                    | 内点最適       | \(\frac{MU_x}{P_x}=\frac{MU_y}{P_y}\)          | At an interior optimum, marginal utility per dollar is equal across goods.                                       |
| Tangency Condition                  | 接点条件       | \(MRS_{xy}=\frac{P_x}{P_y}\)                   | At an interior optimum, the consumer's willingness to substitute equals the market trade-off.                    |
| Indifference Curve                  | 無差別曲線      | \(U(x,y)=\bar U\)                              | Shows combinations of goods providing the same utility.                                                          |
| Marginal Utility per Dollar         | 1ドル当たり限界効用 | \(\frac{MU_x}{P_x}\)                           | Additional utility obtained per dollar spent on a good.                                                          |
| Revealed Preference                 | 顕示選好       | —                                              | Preferences inferred from observed consumer choices.                                                             |

## 4. Income and Substitution Effects

| English             | 日本語    | Formula                                                | Meaning                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Income Effect       | 所得効果   | \(\Delta Q=\Delta Q_{income}\)                         | The change in consumption caused by a change in purchasing power.                                                                           |
| Substitution Effect | 代替効果   | \(\Delta Q=\Delta Q_{substitution}\)                   | The change in consumption caused by a change in relative prices while holding utility or purchasing power conceptually constant.            |
| Total Price Effect  | 総価格効果  | \(\Delta Q=\Delta Q_{substitution}+\Delta Q_{income}\) | The total change in consumption resulting from a price change.                                                                              |
| Normal Good         | 正常財    | \(\frac{\partial Q}{\partial I}>0\)                    | A good for which demand increases as income increases.                                                                                      |
| Inferior Good       | 下級財    | \(\frac{\partial Q}{\partial I}<0\)                    | A good for which demand decreases as income increases.                                                                                      |
| Giffen Good         | ギッフェン財 | —                                                      | A theoretically possible inferior good for which the income effect can dominate the substitution effect, causing demand to rise with price. |

| Isocost Line                            | 等費用線       | \(C=wL+rK\)                               | Shows input combinations with the same total cost.                                               |
| Cost-Minimizing Condition               | 費用最小化条件    | \(\frac{MP_L}{w}=\frac{MP_K}{r}\)         | At an interior cost-minimizing solution, the marginal product per dollar is equal across inputs. |
| Capital-Labor Ratio                     | 資本労働比率     | \(\frac{K}{L}\)                           | The amount of capital used per unit of labor.                                                    |

## 6. Costs

| English                 | 日本語    | Formula                             | Meaning                                                                       |
| ----------------------- | ------ | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fixed Cost              | 固定費    | \(FC\)                              | Cost that does not vary with output in the short run.                         |
| Variable Cost           | 可変費用   | \(VC(Q)\)                           | Cost that varies with output.                                                 |
| Total Cost              | 総費用    | \(TC=FC+VC\)                        | Total production cost.                                                        |
| Average Fixed Cost      | 平均固定費用 | \(AFC=\frac{FC}{Q}\)                | Fixed cost per unit of output.                                                |
| Average Variable Cost   | 平均可変費用 | \(AVC=\frac{VC}{Q}\)                | Variable cost per unit of output.                                             |
| Average Total Cost      | 平均総費用  | \(ATC=\frac{TC}{Q}\)                | Total cost per unit of output.                                                |
| Marginal Cost           | 限界費用   | \(MC=\frac{dTC}{dQ}\)               | Additional cost from producing one additional unit.                           |
| Cost Function           | 費用関数   | \(TC=TC(Q)\)                        | Describes total production cost as a function of output.                      |
| Long-Run Average Cost   | 長期平均費用 | \(LRAC=\frac{LRTC}{Q}\)             | Average cost when all inputs can be adjusted.                                 |
| Short-Run Average Cost  | 短期平均費用 | \(SRAC=\frac{SRTC}{Q}\)             | Average cost when at least one input is fixed.                                |
| Economies of Scale      | 規模の経済  | \(LRAC\downarrow\) as \(Q\uparrow\) | Average cost decreases as output expands in the long run.                     |
| Diseconomies of Scale   | 規模の不経済 | \(LRAC\uparrow\) as \(Q\uparrow\)   | Average cost increases as output expands in the long run.                     |
| Minimum Efficient Scale | 最小効率規模 | \(LRAC=\min\)                       | The smallest output level at which long-run average cost reaches its minimum. |
| Cost Minimization       | 費用最小化  | \(\min_{K,L} C=wL+rK\)              | Finds the least-cost input combination for a given output.                    |

## 7. Revenue and Profit

| English                      | 日本語        | Formula                      | Meaning                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------- | ---------- | ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Total Revenue                | 総収入        | \(TR=P\times Q\)             | Total revenue from sales.                                                                     |
| Average Revenue              | 平均収入       | \(AR=\frac{TR}{Q}\)          | Revenue per unit of output.                                                                   |
| Marginal Revenue             | 限界収入       | \(MR=\frac{dTR}{dQ}\)        | Additional revenue from selling one additional unit.                                          |
| Economic Profit              | 経済的利潤      | \(\pi=TR-TC\)                | Revenue minus both explicit and implicit economic costs.                                      |
| Accounting Profit            | 会計上の利益     | \(TR-\text{Explicit Costs}\) | Revenue minus explicit monetary costs.                                                        |
| Normal Profit                | 正常利潤       | —                            | The return necessary to keep resources in their current use, incorporated into economic cost. |
| Break-Even Point             | 損益分岐点      | \(TR=TC\)                    | The point where economic profit is zero.                                                      |
| Profit Maximization          | 利潤最大化      | \(\max_Q \pi(Q)\)            | The firm's objective of choosing output to maximize profit.                                   |
| First-Order Profit Condition | 利潤最大化の一階条件 | \(MR=MC\)                    | At an interior optimum, marginal revenue equals marginal cost.                                |
| Second-Order Condition       | 二階条件       | \(\frac{d^2\pi}{dQ^2}<0\)    | Ensures that an interior stationary point is a local maximum.                                 |
| Shutdown Condition           | 操業停止条件     | \(P<AVC\)                    | In the short run, a price below minimum AVC means the firm cannot cover variable costs.       |
| Zero Economic Profit         | 経済的利潤ゼロ    | \(TR=TC\)                    | The firm covers all economic costs, including opportunity costs.                              |

## Core Microeconomics Relationships

The following relationships are especially important:

$$
Q_d=Q_s
$$

$$
MB=MC
$$

$$
MR=MC
$$

$$
\frac{MU_x}{P_x}=\frac{MU_y}{P_y}
$$

$$
MRS_{xy}=\frac{P_x}{P_y}
$$

$$
\frac{MP_L}{w}=\frac{MP_K}{r}
$$

$$
MSC=MSB
$$

$$
MRP_L=w
$$

These equations represent different optimization or equilibrium conditions:

* **Market equilibrium:** \(Q_d=Q_s\)
* **Efficient quantity:** \(MB=MC\)
* **Profit maximization:** \(MR=MC\)
* **Consumer optimization:** \(\frac{MU_x}{P_x}=\frac{MU_y}{P_y}\)
* **Cost minimization:** \(\frac{MP_L}{w}=\frac{MP_K}{r}\)
* **Social efficiency:** \(MSC=MSB\)
* **Labor demand:** \(MRP_L=w\)
