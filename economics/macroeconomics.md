# Macroeconomics — Fundamental Formulas and Concepts

This file provides a mathematical foundation for macroeconomics.
It focuses on national income accounting, economic growth, inflation, unemployment, money, fiscal policy, monetary policy, business cycles, international economics, and aggregate demand and supply.

## 1. National Income Accounting

| English                        | 日本語        | Formula                                          | Meaning                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------ | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Gross Domestic Product (GDP)   | 国内総生産      | \(GDP=C+I+G+NX\)                                 | The market value of final goods and services produced within a country's borders during a given period.                |
| Consumption                    | 消費         | \(C\)                                            | Household spending on goods and services.                                                                              |
| Investment                     | 投資         | \(I\)                                            | Spending on capital goods, inventories, and residential structures.                                                    |
| Government Purchases           | 政府購入       | \(G\)                                            | Government spending on currently produced goods and services.                                                          |
| Net Exports                    | 純輸出        | \(NX=X-M\)                                       | Exports minus imports.                                                                                                 |
| Exports                        | 輸出         | \(X\)                                            | Goods and services sold to foreign buyers.                                                                             |
| Imports                        | 輸入         | \(M\)                                            | Goods and services purchased from foreign producers.                                                                   |
| Nominal GDP                    | 名目GDP      | \(GDP_N=\sum_i P_{i,t}Q_{i,t}\)                  | GDP valued at current-period prices.                                                                                   |
| Real GDP                       | 実質GDP      | \(GDP_R=\sum_i P_{i,base}Q_{i,t}\)               | GDP measured using base-period prices.                                                                                 |
| GDP Deflator                   | GDPデフレーター  | \(\frac{Nominal\ GDP}{Real\ GDP}\times100\)      | An index of the price level for domestically produced final goods and services.                                        |
| GDP per Capita                 | 一人当たりGDP   | \(\frac{GDP}{Population}\)                       | GDP divided by population.                                                                                             |
| Real GDP per Capita            | 一人当たり実質GDP | \(\frac{Real\ GDP}{Population}\)                 | Real output per person.                                                                                                |
| National Income                | 国民所得       | —                                                | Income earned by factors of production in an economy, with definitions depending on the accounting framework.          |
| Gross National Product (GNP)   | 国民総生産      | \(GNP=GDP+\text{Net Factor Income from Abroad}\) | Production attributable to a country's residents or nationals, depending on the accounting framework.                  |
| Net Domestic Product (NDP)     | 国内純生産      | \(NDP=GDP-Depreciation\)                         | GDP minus depreciation of capital.                                                                                     |
| Depreciation                   | 減価償却       | —                                                | The decline in the value of capital used in production.                                                                |
| Disposable Income              | 可処分所得      | \(Y_d=Y-T+TR\)                                   | Income available to households after taxes and including transfers.                                                    |
| Private Saving                 | 民間貯蓄       | \(S_p=Y-T-C\)                                    | Household and business income not spent on consumption or taxes, in a simplified national-income framework.            |
| Public Saving                  | 政府貯蓄       | \(S_g=T-G-TR\)                                   | Government revenue minus government purchases and transfers.                                                           |
| National Saving                | 国民貯蓄       | \(S=S_p+S_g\)                                    | Total domestic saving.                                                                                                 |
| Saving-Investment Identity     | 貯蓄・投資恒等式   | \(S=I+NX\)                                       | In an open economy, national saving equals domestic investment plus net exports under standard accounting definitions. |
| Closed-Economy Saving Identity | 閉鎖経済の貯蓄恒等式 | \(S=I\)                                          | In a closed economy, national saving equals domestic investment.                                                       |

## 2. Economic Growth

| English                   | 日本語        | Formula                                      | Meaning                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------- | ---------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Economic Growth           | 経済成長       | \(g_Y=\frac{Y_t-Y_{t-1}}{Y_{t-1}}\times100\) | The percentage change in real output over time.                                                                |
| Per Capita Growth         | 一人当たり成長率   | \(g_{Y/N}\approx g_Y-g_N\)                   | Approximate growth in output per person.                                                                       |
| Compound Growth           | 複利成長       | \(Y_t=Y_0(1+g)^t\)                           | Growth in which each period's growth is applied to the accumulated level.                                      |
| Continuous Growth         | 連続成長       | \(Y_t=Y_0e^{gt}\)                            | Growth represented using a continuously compounded growth rate.                                                |
| Rule of 70                | 70の法則      | \(Doubling\ Time\approx\frac{70}{g}\)        | Approximate years required for a variable to double when the annual growth rate is \(g\%\).                    |
| Production Function       | 生産関数       | \(Y=F(K,L,A)\)                               | Describes aggregate output as a function of capital, labor, and technology/productivity.                       |
| Capital per Worker        | 労働者一人当たり資本 | \(k=\frac{K}{L}\)                            | Capital available per unit of labor.                                                                           |
| Output per Worker         | 労働者一人当たり産出 | \(y=\frac{Y}{L}\)                            | Output produced per worker.                                                                                    |
| Total Factor Productivity | 全要素生産性     | \(A=\frac{Y}{F(K,L)}\)                       | A measure of productivity not directly explained by measured factor inputs in a production-function framework. |
| Capital Accumulation      | 資本蓄積       | \(\Delta K=I-\delta K\)                      | Change in the capital stock equals investment minus depreciation.                                              |
| Depreciation Rate         | 減価償却率      | \(\delta=\frac{Depreciation}{K}\)            | The fraction of the capital stock that depreciates during a period.                                            |
| Steady State              | 定常状態       | \(\Delta k=0\)                               | A state in which capital per worker is constant in the relevant growth model.                                  |
| Solow Production Function | ソロー生産関数    | \(y=f(k)\)                                   | Output per worker as a function of capital per worker in the Solow model.                                      |
| Golden Rule Capital Stock | 黄金律資本ストック  | \(MPK=\delta+n+g\)                           | In the standard Solow framework, the capital level that maximizes steady-state consumption per worker.         |
| Labor Productivity        | 労働生産性      | \(\frac{Y}{L}\)                              | Output per unit of labor.                                                                                      |
| Productivity Growth       | 生産性成長      | \(g_A=\frac{A_t-A_{t-1}}{A_{t-1}}\)          | Growth in measured total factor productivity.                                                                  |

| Inflation Expectations      | インフレ期待      | \(E_t[\pi_{t+1}]\)                                                  | Expected future inflation formed by economic agents.                                                                   |
| Inflation Tax               | インフレ税       | —                                                                   | The reduction in the real value of money balances associated with inflation.                                           |

| ------------------------------ | ---------- | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| Balanced Budget Multiplier     | 均衡予算乗数     | \(k_{BB}=1\)                        | In the simplest Keynesian model, equal increases in government spending and taxes increase equilibrium output by the amount of the spending increase. |
| Investment Multiplier          | 投資乗数       | \(k_I=\frac{1}{1-MPC}\)             | Measures the output response to an autonomous change in investment in the simple model.                                                               |
| Government Spending Multiplier | 政府支出乗数     | \(k_G=\frac{1}{1-MPC}\)             | Measures the output response to an autonomous change in government purchases in the simple model.                                                     |
| Disposable Income              | 可処分所得      | \(Y_d=Y-T+TR\)                      | Household income available for consumption and saving.                                                                                                |

## Core Macroeconomic Relationships

The following relationships form a useful foundation for introductory macroeconomics:

$$
Y=C+I+G+NX
$$

$$
S=I+NX
$$

$$
S_p=Y-T+TR-C
$$

$$
S_g=T-G-TR
$$

$$
S=S_p+S_g
$$

$$
\pi=\frac{P_t-P_{t-1}}{P_{t-1}}\times100
$$

$$
u=\frac{U}{LF}\times100
$$

$$
LFPR=\frac{LF}{Working\text{-}Age\ Population}\times100
$$

$$
r\approx i-\pi
$$

$$
MV=PY
$$

$$
Y=AE
$$

$$
k=\frac{1}{1-MPC}
$$

$$
\Delta K=I-\delta K
$$

$$
Y=F(K,L,A)
$$

$$
\frac{Y-Y^*}{Y^*}\times100=Output\ Gap
$$

These relationships connect the major areas of macroeconomics:

* **National income accounting:** \(Y=C+I+G+NX\)
* **Saving and investment:** \(S=I+NX\)
* **Inflation:** \(\pi=\frac{\Delta P}{P}\)
* **Unemployment:** \(u=\frac{U}{LF}\)
* **Money:** \(MV=PY\)
* **Keynesian expenditure:** \(Y=AE\)
* **Economic growth:** \(Y=F(K,L,A)\)
* **Capital accumulation:** \(\Delta K=I-\delta K\)
* **Monetary policy:** \(i,\ r,\ \pi\)
* **Fiscal policy:** \(G,\ T,\ TR\)
* **International macroeconomics:** \(NX,\ CA,\ NCO,\ E\)
